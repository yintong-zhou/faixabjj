# Brand Guidelines — FAIXA BJJ

> Generated with Brand Spec Sheet on 9/10/2026.

## The project

- **Name**: FAIXA BJJ
- **Description**: tracking students' technical progression toward stripes and belts — with a simple data model designed to add minimal workload for instructors.
- **Target audience**: Gym with brasilian master or italian istructors about 30-50 years old
- **Sector**: Tech / Software
- **Mood**: Minimal

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Primary | `#16181D` |
| Secondary | `#F4F5F7` |
| Accent | `#3457D5` |
| Dark neutral | `#3A3F4B` |
| Light neutral | `#E3E6EB` |

## Typography

- **Headings**: Sora, weight 700
- **Body**: Work Sans, weight 400
- Keep body copy around 60–70 characters per line for readability.

## Tone of voice

`essential` · `clear` · `calm` · `precise` · `no-frills`

## Imagery & iconography

Clean photography with plenty of negative space, a desaturated palette, simple geometric compositions. Avoid superfluous decoration or too many subjects in a single frame. Add clean product screenshots and abstract geometric illustrations to explain technical concepts.

## Spacing & grid

- **Base unit**: 8px
- **Scale**: 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 96px
- **Corner radius**: 4–8px
- 12-column grid with generous margins: lots of white space between sections.

## Logo — clear space

The logo lives in isolation: minimum clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side. One color per application, no shadows or effects.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Let elements breathe
- Use only one accent color at a time
- Align everything to a consistent grid
- Show the real product (screenshots, UI) instead of abstract metaphors

**Avoid:**
- Mix more than two typefaces
- Fill empty space for its own sake
- Add decorative shadows or gradients
- Use generic clip art or stock icons seen everywhere else
